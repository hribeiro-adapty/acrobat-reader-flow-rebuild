# Growth Audit — Adobe Acrobat Reader

**Live:** https://hribeiro-adapty.github.io/acrobat-reader-flow-rebuild/
**Prototype on a phone:** https://hribeiro-adapty.github.io/acrobat-reader-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic you already have, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Acrobat Reader's own design. Prepared by Adapty, based on the live iOS flow and the App Store listing.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Let people in before asking for an account | Welcome, Sign in | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Ask what they came to do, and answer it | What they need, Where it lives | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Open the paywall on the job they came for | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Put your 4.6 from 656K ratings next to the price | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Offer one month when they close the paywall | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |

The prototype carries a Light / Dark switch, English and Brazilian Portuguese, a 30-second walkthrough, and jump chips so you can open any screen with answers already filled in.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, install count and feature row comes from Acrobat Reader's own published material: the App Store listing (US and Brazil storefronts) and the in-app onboarding and paywall screens. Nothing is invented; there is no new discount, only prices Acrobat already sells.

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Adobe Acrobat Reader.*
